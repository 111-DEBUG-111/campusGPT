Rishihood University Internship and Placement
Policy
0. Three Principles to Follow
1. Be Professional
2. Communicate Early
3. Represent Rishihood with Dignity
1. Dress Code (Internship, Placements, Corporate Activities)
Western Formals
Boys
● White formal full-sleeves shirt
● Black formal trousers
● Black belt
● Black lace-up shoes, black socks
● Formal tie
● Clean-shaven (moustache allowed)
● Sikh students: Black or navy turban
Girls
● White formal full-sleeves shirt
● Black formal trousers
● Black formal shoes (heel up to 2 inches)
● Hair neatly tied with a black hair-tie
B-Suit (Boys & Girls)
● Plain black suit
● Boys: Two-button blazer
● Girls: Blazer with at least one functional button
Indian Formals (Approved Alternatives)
● Boys: Off-white or cream kurta with formal churidar/trousers, optional Nehru
jacket
● Girls: Cotton/silk saree or formal salwar-kameez with dupatta
● Footwear must be formal and sober
● No bright colours, prints, or casual wear
2. Internship Requirements
● Internship is mandatory for programme completion.
● Each student will have a faculty guide and must stay in periodic contact.
● Students may work individually or in a team, but each student must submit an
individual report and presentation.
● Joint projects must be reported to the Placement Office within one week with
supervisor confirmation.
● Submit internship report (PDF) to the Placement Office.
● Presentations will be scheduled by the Academic Office.
3. Internship Process Rules
● Once registered for an opportunity, attendance for the full selection process is
required.
● It is mandatory to accept the offer in case a student is selected by a
company for Internship through the Placement office. Failing to do so would
result in debarment from Final Placements.
● Missing any stage counts as a violation.
● Students with self-secured internships must inform the Placement Office
immediately.
● Communication with companies must always go through the Placement Office.
● Any misconduct (late arrival, dress code violation, missing deadlines,
unprofessional behaviour) will attract penalties as per the University’s disciplinary
framework.
● Students may not negotiate role, location, or stipend directly with companies.
● Sick leave requires informing the Placement Office at least 4 hours in advance and
providing a medical certificate.
4. Final Placement Policies
Eligibility
● Minimum CGPA 6.0
● No F grades
● No cheating or disciplinary cases
Registration
● Submit resume and information form in prescribed format by the deadline.
● Missing deadlines leads to temporary ineligibility.
Attempts
● Students may apply to up to 10 companies.
● Any evaluated step counts as one attempt.
● Attendance for all stages is mandatory. Once selected the offer will be binding.
Conduct
● Professional behaviour is always required: punctuality, proper attire, active
participation.
● No direct negotiation with companies.
● Negative feedback from a company may lead to temporary suspension from
further processes.
Job Offers
● Students may accept only one final offer.
● If multiple offers arise, the first offer must be accepted.
● All communication before joining must happen through the Placement Office.
PPO/PPI
● Students must report any PPO/PPI.
● PPOs at or above the university’s average salary must be accepted.
● Students with PPOs may apply to two additional companies offering higher
compensation.
5. Mandatory Engagements
● Attendance is required for all placement-related activities: Batch Meets, Guest
Lectures, Leadership Talks, corporate interactions.
● Students in regular classes at the same time are exempt.
6. Seminar Course (Guest Lecture) Requirements
● Minimum 20 points before internship selection.
● Minimum 40 points before final placement selection.
● Points:
○ 2 points for each 90-120 minutes seminar
○ +1 point for every extra 60 minutes
● Activities include faculty seminars, corporate lectures, leadership talks, and
approved events.
7. Discipline and Misconduct
● Repeated violations, unprofessional conduct, sharing confidential information, or
interference by family members will lead to disciplinary action including
temporary or complete debarment from placement support.
● The University may revise policies based on circumstances.
● All final decisions rest with the University.
These rules may be revised at the University’s discretion based on circumstances. All
University decisions are final and binding.
